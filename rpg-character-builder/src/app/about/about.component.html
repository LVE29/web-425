Firefly Forest Character Builder is a place to begin creating your own fantasy character. Follow the fireflies through the forest, explore the available character classes, and discover the abilities, strengths, and weaknesses that make each character unique.

How Your Adventure Begins

  1. 1

    Choose a Character Class

    Visit Character Classes and choose the character that interests you. Each class has its own appearance, magical abilities, strengths, and weaknesses.

  2. 2

    Discover Your Character

    Open the character’s profile to learn more about who they are and what they can do. Use those details as the beginning of a backstory or future adventure.

  3. 3

    Let the Dice Decide

    Visit the Ability Roller and roll a six-sided die. The result adds an element of chance that can help shape an ability, decision, or moment in your character’s story.

There is no single correct path through Firefly Forest. Choose the character that speaks to you, follow the glowing trail, and let your imagination decide where the story goes next.
